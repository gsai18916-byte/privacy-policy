This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Arrow Puzzle mobile game ("the Game"). The Game is published by Moonglue Studios.

1. Information and advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information from your device, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • Device and advertising identifiers;
  • Information about ad views and interactions with the Game;
  • Diagnostics and performance information, such as app launch or SDK performance data.

This information may be used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and improving advertising services. The information collected can depend on your device, settings, consent choices, and the version and configuration of the advertising SDK.

2. How we use information

We use Google AdMob in the Game to serve and measure advertisements and support the operation of the Game. We do not use the Game to ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service. Google may process information as described in its own privacy documentation and applicable terms. Please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

We do not control Google's independent data practices. Any advertising partners or services enabled through your AdMob configuration should also be reflected in this policy.

4. Advertising choices

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be shown a consent message and may be able to choose whether to receive personalized advertising. You can also review or reset your device's advertising ID and manage ad privacy settings in your Android device settings. Available controls may vary by device and Android version.

5. Data security and retention

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Information collected by Google is handled and retained according to Google's policies and settings. We do not independently store AdMob advertising identifiers or SDK data on our own servers.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. If the Game is intended for children, or is included in a child-directed or mixed-audience program, its audience settings, ad serving configuration, and this policy must be updated to meet applicable laws and Google Play Families requirements.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its advertising setup, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
